who I am

About Lotfi

I'm an engineering student in Algeria, graduating this year in network and embedded systems. Along the way I fell in love with building software, first small projects, then backends, then full products for clients and my own ideas.

Building made me curious about how things break. That curiosity pulled me toward cybersecurity, which I'm now learning properly through labs, CTFs and practice.

I share what I learn in Darija because I wish I had found this kind of content earlier. I'm not an expert yet, and I won't pretend to be. Come learn with me.
